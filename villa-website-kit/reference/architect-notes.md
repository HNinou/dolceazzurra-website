# Architect notes (paraphrased)

Source: Giordano Hadamik Architects, "Villa NN - Dolce & Azzurra": https://www.gha4u.com/villa-nn
The architects' original text is their copyright. Paraphrase it. Quote at most one short sentence, credited and linked.

## The idea
- The house was designed to respect the site first: to become part of the landscape rather than dominate it.
- It sits among ancient olive trees on the Alassio hillside and follows the natural contours of the land.
- Every decision aimed at a dialogue between architecture and nature, with minimal impact on the site.

## Layout
- Single level, simple and intuitive.
- Open-plan living area framed by floor-to-ceiling glass: living room, kitchen, dining and terrace merge into one space.
- Bedrooms in a lateral wing for privacy, quiet and uninterrupted views.
- At the centre: an open-air courtyard under a timber pergola. It links the two wings, moves you between inside and outside,
  and shelters from summer sun and prevailing winds, creating its own microclimate.

## Materials
- Local stone, warm-toned plaster and timber, matching the olive groves and the colours of the coast.
- Generous roof overhangs, covered terraces and the pergola give shade for outdoor living through the seasons.

## Outdoors
- The infinity pool is the focal point of the landscape; a large, private sun deck with views.
- A lounge with fireplace under the olive trees for summer evenings.
- Table tennis and a traditional bocce court.

## Sustainability (as the architects describe it; no certification)
- Orientation, natural cross-ventilation, a green roof, minimal sealing of the ground.
- The owner confirms: no photovoltaics yet; solar panels for hot water only.

## Recognition
- Longlisted: Small House of the Year (Rural), Archello Awards 2026.
- Project year on the architect's page: 2024 (construction 2024-2026, completed 2026).
