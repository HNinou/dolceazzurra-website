# Strategy summary (from the website & marketing proposal, 26 Oct 2026)

## Positioning
Market Dolce & Azzurra as a **design villa by named architects**: "an architect's house among olive trees, with the whole bay of Alassio in front of it". Most Alassio villas compete on "sea view + pool"; few can show award-listed architecture.

## Audience
- Design-minded couples and friends: DE, CH, NL, UK.
- Multigenerational families: DE, CH, NL, FR (single level, 4 equal suites, pool safety cover, 10 min to the beach).
- Italian and French short stays in shoulder season.
- Remote workers in winter (Starlink, mild climate).

## Website principles
- Calm, photo-led, editorial; one job per page; booking one click from everywhere.
- Each language at its own URL (`/fr/`, `/it/` …). EN, FR, IT at launch; DE and NL after native review.
- Hostaway calendar widget is the only booking path.
- No hidden SEO text, no keyword lists, no feature-spec overload (specs live in a tidy amenities list).
- Facts must be consistent everywhere: completed 2026; passive-house *principles* (not certified); solar hot water (no PV); award = longlist.

## Design direction
Stone, oak, light and space. Palette from the photos: warm plaster `#F4EFE8`, olive-black `#23251F`, bay blue `#2F5D7C`, oak `#C9A67C`, olive `#7D8461`, dusk rose `#D9B7AE`. Serif headings (Fraunces) + Inter text, self-hosted. Hero: the pergola framing the bay (photo 01).

## Marketing (context for copy, not for the site build)
Architecture PR (Archello, Dezeen, ArchDaily, German design press), curated listings (Urlaubsarchitektur, Plum Guide), Google Vacation Rentals via Hostaway, Instagram, rewritten platform listings, a guest e-mail list for direct rebooking.
