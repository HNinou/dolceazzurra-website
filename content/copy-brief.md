# Copy brief: Villa Dolce & Azzurra

English is the source language. Write EN first, then FR and IT (the owner proofreads these three).
Write DE and NL drafts only when asked; they stay `status: draft` until a native speaker has reviewed them.

## Positioning (one line)

An architect's house among olive trees, with the whole bay of Alassio in front of it.

## Who reads this

- Design-minded couples and groups of friends from Germany, Switzerland, the Netherlands and the UK.
- Multigenerational families (single level, 4 equal suites, pool safety cover, 10 min to the beach).
- French and Italian guests for long weekends and shoulder season.
- Remote workers in autumn and winter (Starlink, mild climate).

## Voice

- Quiet, precise, warm. Closer to an architecture magazine than a holiday catalogue.
- Short sentences. Concrete details: numbers, materials, minutes, names.
- Show, don't claim: "the bay fills the kitchen window", not "breathtaking views".
- Use "you" for the guest. Use "we" for the hosts.
- Northern European readers distrust hype. Never oversell.

## Key messages, in order

1. **The view**: 180° over the bay, from the pool, the patio and the kitchen.
2. **The architecture**: stone, oak, timber and olive trees. Built into the land, not on it.
   Designed by Giordano Hadamik Architects; longlisted, Archello Awards 2026, Small House of the Year (Rural).
3. **The ease**: one level, 4 equal suites, everything included, 10 minutes to the beach and the Budello.

## Banned claims (any language). The verifier fails the build on these.

| Never write | Why | Write instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passivhaus certified / certification / zertifiziert | Not certified | "Designed on passive-house principles" |
| Photovoltaic, PV, solar power %, "80% solar" | No PV; only solar hot water | "Solar panels for hot water" |
| "25 kWh/m²/year" or any energy figure | Not measured | Describe the features (green roof, thermal mass, cross-ventilation) |
| 5-star / 5 étoiles / 5 stelle / 5-Sterne | Not a rated hotel | "Hotel-quality bedding", specific details |
| Award winner / awarded / prize | It's a longlist | "Longlisted, Archello Awards 2026" |
| Paradise, breathtaking, stunning, unique, dream | Hype | Concrete detail |
| Mantellassi or any agency | One booking path only | Hostaway widget / "Check dates" |
| Invented reviews or testimonials | Illegal and dishonest | Real quotes from `villa.yaml` only |
| Salt-water pool | Not confirmed | "Infinity pool" |
